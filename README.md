## About

I am a Brazilian Product Designer working across UX/UI, branding, web experiences, and design systems. I like turning ambiguous ideas into clear, useful products—from research and product strategy to interface design, prototypes, and implementation-ready documentation.

I co-founded **PROJECT/X**, an independent studio that partners with teams to create brands, digital products, high-performance websites, and innovation initiatives. I also work as a Product Designer at the **Bahian Institute for Sustainable Development (IDSB)**.

My practice combines visual craft with systems thinking, using AI and automation when they genuinely improve the way people build and use products.

## Focus areas

- **Product experience** — User-centered flows, wireframes, interfaces, prototyping, and usability-informed iteration.
- **Brand systems** — Visual identities and scalable systems that make products recognizable and consistent.
- **Digital platforms** — Responsive websites and CMS-driven experiences that balance performance, clarity, and expression.
- **Innovation practice** — Design thinking, strategic consulting, and structured workflows for teams moving from idea to delivery.
- **AI-enabled workflows** — Practical use of AI agents, automation, and documentation to make creative and product work more effective.
